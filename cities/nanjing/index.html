Nanjing, China — capital of Jiangsu Province (80 million people) and one of China's most historically significant cities — is home to Nanjing Drum Tower Hospital (南京鼓楼医院), one of the country's most respected institutions for spinal surgery and orthopedics. Nanjing's hospitals offer excellent care at very competitive prices, in a city known for its beautiful Purple Mountain, Ming dynasty tomb sites, and the historic Presidential Palace.

Nanjing occupies a strategic position as eastern China's major medical hub between Shanghai and Beijing. Patients who find Shanghai too expensive or Beijing too far often find Nanjing offers an ideal balance — top-tier specialists, modern facilities, and significantly lower costs than both of the larger cities.

Why Medical Tourists Choose Nanjing

Nanjing's medical reputation centers on its exceptional spinal surgery and orthopedic programs at Drum Tower Hospital, combined with general medical care that rivals larger cities at more accessible prices.

Top Hospitals That Accept Foreign Patients

Nanjing Drum Tower Hospital — 南京鼓楼医院

📍 321 Zhongshan Road, Nanjing ☎️ +86-25-8310-6666 🌐 www.njglyy.com 🗂 Tertiary / National Spine Surgery Leader 🏥 Founded 1892

Specialties: Spinal Surgery, Orthopedics, Joint Replacement, Neurosurgery, Cardiology, Oncology, Gastroenterology, Respiratory Medicine

International Services: International Medical Center with English coordinators. One of China's most experienced hospitals with foreign patients. Translation services available. Accepts direct overseas appointments.

📋 Note: Drum Tower Hospital's spinal surgery department is nationally renowned — if you need spinal fusion, disc surgery, or complex spine treatment, this is one of the best destinations in Asia. Also one of China's oldest hospitals (founded 1892 as a missionary hospital).

Jiangsu Province People's Hospital — 江苏省人民医院

📍 300 Guangzhou Road, Nanjing ☎️ +86-25-8378-9999 🌐 www.jsph.net.cn 🗂 Tertiary / Provincial Flagship 🏥 Founded 1934

Specialties: Cardiology, Cardiac Surgery, Oncology, Nephrology, Neurology, Emergency Medicine, Respiratory Medicine, Hematology

International Services: International Patient Services with English coordinators. Jiangsu Province's largest and most comprehensive hospital. Accepts foreign patients for all major specialties.

📋 Note: The flagship hospital of Jiangsu Province. Strong cardiac care program including bypass surgery and interventional cardiology. Located near Nanjing's main commercial district — Xinjiekou.

Eastern Theater General Hospital — 东部战区总医院

📍 305 East Zhongshan Road, Nanjing ☎️ +86-25-8086-1000 🌐 www.njzyy.com 🗂 Tertiary / Military Flagship 🏥 Founded 1929

Specialties: Trauma Surgery, Critical Care, Cardiology, Neurology, General Surgery, Emergency Medicine, Organ Transplantation Support

International Services: International liaison office. High-volume surgical center with exceptional emergency and critical care capabilities. Accepts foreign patients for consultations and treatment.

📋 Note: Nanjing's most capable hospital for trauma and emergency cases. Strong surgical infrastructure from military background. Good option for complex surgical cases and post-trauma recovery.

Nanjing Medical University First Affiliated Hospital — 南京医科大学第一附属医院

📍 300 Guangzhou Road, Nanjing ☎️ +86-25-6813-5000 🗂 Tertiary / Teaching Hospital 🏥 Founded 1934

Specialties: Oncology, Cardiology, Hepatobiliary Surgery, Nephrology, Respiratory Medicine, Medical Imaging, Interventional Radiology

International Services: International Patient Services with English coordinators. Well-regarded oncology program with comprehensive cancer care including surgery, chemotherapy, and radiotherapy.

📋 Note: Strong in cancer treatment and cardiology. Located in the same area as Jiangsu Province People's Hospital — central Nanjing with many hotel options nearby.

While You Wait — Explore the City

Many procedures and diagnostic tests (MRI, CT, blood work) can be scheduled within 3–7 days. While you wait for results or pre-procedure assessments, you can explore the city. Your hospital liaison office can help plan sightseeing between appointments.

How to pay:

Most hospitals accept cash (RMB), UnionPay cards, and major credit cards. WeChat Pay and Alipay require a Chinese bank account.

How to Schedule an Appointment

Option 1: Contact the hospital's international office directly

  1. Find the international/liaison contact in the Hospital section above
  2. Email or call to request an appointment — for spinal/orthopedic cases, include imaging descriptions
  3. Bring your passport to registration

Option 2: Through a medical facilitator

Facilitators in Nanjing can help with appointment coordination and translation for major surgical cases. Fees typically $150–350.

⚠️ What to bring to your appointment:

  • Passport (required)
  • For spinal/orthopedic cases: bring all imaging CDs (MRI, CT, X-ray) and written reports
  • All previous medical records
  • List of current medications

Getting to Nanjing from the US

Nanjing Lukou International Airport (NJG) has limited direct US flights. Most travelers connect through Shanghai, Seoul, Tokyo, or Hong Kong.

RouteFlight DurationRound-Trip Economy
LAX → NJG (via SHA/ICN)~15–18 hours total$650–1,200
SFO → NJG (via Seoul)~14–17 hours total$600–1,100
SEA → NJG (via Tokyo)~14–16 hours total$600–1,100

From the airport to the city:

  • Metro Line S1 (机场地铁S1号线) — Connects Nanjing Lukou Airport to central Nanjing (~40 minutes to Nanjing South Station). ¥6–12.
  • Taxi — ¥100–180 to central Nanjing. About 40–60 minutes.
  • Didi — Request via app once you have data.

High-speed rail connections from Shanghai/Beijing:

  • Shanghai → Nanjing: ~60 minutes (G-train)
  • Beijing → Nanjing: ~3.5 hours (G-train)
  • Hangzhou → Nanjing: ~70 minutes (G-train)

Visa:

US citizens need a tourist visa (L visa). Apply at the Chinese Visa Application Service Center. Processing: 4–5 business days. Cost: $140.

Money & Payments

Currency: Chinese Yuan (RMB / ¥)

  • Cash: RMB works everywhere
  • Cards: Visa and Mastercard accepted at most hospitals and hotels
  • WeChat Pay & Alipay: Most convenient — Chinese bank account recommended

Exchange money:

  • Best rates: ATM withdrawals with Charles Schwab, Revolut, or Wise debit cards
  • Airport: Available but poor rates
  • Banks: ICBC and China Construction Bank near hospitals offer fair rates

Cost of living reference (Nanjing):

  • Cheap meal: ¥15–35 ($2–5)
  • Mid-range restaurant: ¥60–150 ($9–22)
  • Subway: ¥2–8 ($0.30–1.20)
  • Taxi (per km): ¥2–3.5 ($0.30–0.50)

Staying Connected in China

Internet: China uses blocked internet. Install a VPN before arrival.

Before you leave the US:

  1. Install a VPN on all devices
  2. Download offline maps from Maps.me or Google Maps
  3. Download Google Translate with offline Chinese language pack
  4. Download WeChat and register your phone number

SIM cards at Nanjing airport:

Buy a Chinese SIM card at NJG from China Mobile, China Unicom, or China Telecom. Tourist packages: ¥80–200 for 15–30 days with data.

Useful apps:

  • WeChat — essential
  • Didi — ride-hailing (works with foreign phone)
  • Pleco — Chinese dictionary
  • Google Translate — offline language pack
  • Maps.me — offline maps

Important Tips for First-Time Visitors

Ready to Get Started?

Contact Drum Tower Hospital's international office directly for spinal surgery and orthopedic consultations. Nanjing offers top-tier care at more accessible prices than Shanghai or Beijing.

Before you go:

  • Apply for your Chinese tourist visa (L visa)
  • Notify your bank you'll be in China
  • Install and test a VPN before arrival
  • Download WeChat and register your phone number
  • For spinal/orthopedic cases: bring all imaging CDs and reports
  • Bring all medical records
  • Save hospital addresses in Chinese characters
  • Bring a debit card with no foreign ATM fees

At the hospital:

  • Bring your passport — required
  • Arrive 30–60 minutes early
  • Keep all receipts and medical records
  • Ask for English-language summaries of your treatment plan

🚨 Emergencies:

  • Ambulance: 120
  • Police: 110

After you return home:

  • Keep all medical records and imaging CDs
  • Follow up with your US doctor
  • Contact Nanjing hospitals directly for follow-up needs

About Nanjing's Hospitals

Nanjing Drum Tower Hospital (南京鼓楼医院) and Jiangsu Province People's Hospital are among East China's most respected medical institutions. Drum Tower Hospital has a strong reputation in spinal surgery, cardiology, and oncology, serving patients from across Jiangsu Province and beyond.

Nanjing's medical community has deep roots in traditional Chinese medicine as well as modern Western medicine, making it a good choice for patients interested in integrated treatment approaches combining both systems.
