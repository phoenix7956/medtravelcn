Chengdu, China — the capital of Sichuan Province (83 million people) and one of China's most livable cities — is home to West China Hospital (华西医院), consistently ranked among the top 2 hospitals in all of China. Foreign patients increasingly come to Chengdu for its exceptional medical depth, particularly in neurology, neurosurgery, oral/dental surgery, and complex diagnostics.

Chengdu also offers a uniquely comfortable medical tourism experience: the city is famous for its laid-back culture, exceptional food, giant panda research center, and welcoming atmosphere. Patients can recover in one of China's most pleasant cities while receiving care from world-class specialists.

Why Medical Tourists Choose Chengdu

Chengdu's medical reputation is built on West China Hospital — a name that commands respect throughout Asia. The hospital system associated with Sichuan University's medical school is China's single largest hospital system by bed count and handles some of the most complex cases in the country.

What makes Chengdu different:

  • West China Hospital (华西医院) — Ranked China's #2 hospital overall; particularly renowned for neurology, neurosurgery, oral/dental surgery, and internal medicine. One of the world's highest-volume medical centers.
  • Dental and oral surgery — West China Hospital's stomatology (dental) department is among the best in Asia; dental implants, full-mouth restoration, and oral surgery at a fraction of US costs
  • Neurology and neurosurgery — West China has dedicated institutes for brain science, treating stroke, epilepsy, Parkinson's, and brain tumors with exceptional expertise
  • 2025: Foreign patient visits grew 61% year-over-year; many patients come specifically for dental work and neurological consultations
  • Chengdu is one of China's most pleasant cities — great food, pandas, and a relaxed culture make recovery more comfortable than larger cities

While You Wait — Explore the City

Many procedures and diagnostic tests (MRI, CT, blood work) can be scheduled within 3–7 days. While you wait for results or pre-procedure assessments, you can explore the city. Your hospital liaison office can help plan sightseeing between appointments.

How to pay:

Most hospitals accept cash (RMB), UnionPay cards, and major credit cards. WeChat Pay and Alipay require a Chinese bank account.

How to Schedule an Appointment

Option 1: Contact the hospital's international office directly

  1. Find the international/liaison contact in the Hospital section above
  2. Email or call to request an appointment — include your diagnosis and medical summary
  3. Bring your passport to registration
  4. Arrive 30–60 minutes early on the day of your appointment

Option 2: Through a medical facilitator

Facilitators in Chengdu can help with appointment coordination, translation, and logistics. Fees typically $150–350.

⚠️ What to bring to your appointment:

  • Passport (required)
  • All previous medical records and imaging CDs
  • List of current medications
  • Any dental records or oral imaging if seeking dental treatment

Language tips:

  • Download Google Translate with offline Chinese language pack
  • Pleco dictionary for medical terminology
  • WeChat has built-in translation
  • West China Hospital has dedicated English coordinators for international patients

Getting to Chengdu from the US

Chengdu Tianfu International Airport (TFU) opened in 2021 as Chengdu's main international gateway. Chengdu Shuangliu International Airport (CTU) now handles mostly domestic flights. Most international flights from the US route through Seoul, Tokyo, or other Asian hubs.

RouteFlight DurationRound-Trip Economy
LAX → TFU (via ICN/NRT)~17–20 hours total$700–1,300
SFO → TFU (via Seoul)~16–19 hours total$650–1,200
SEA → TFU (via Tokyo)~15–18 hours total$650–1,200

From the airports to the city:

  • Chengdu Tianfu (TFU) — Metro Lines 10/18 connect to central Chengdu (~45–60 minutes to city center). Taxi: ¥100–180.
  • Chengdu Shuangliu (CTU) — Metro Line 10 connects to city center (~30 minutes). Closer to central Chengdu than TFU. Taxi: ¥40–80.
  • Didi — Request via app once you have data.

Visa:

US citizens need a tourist visa (L visa). Apply at the Chinese Visa Application Service Center. Processing: 4–5 business days. Cost: $140.

Staying Connected in China

Internet: China uses blocked internet. Install a VPN before arrival.

Before you leave the US:

  1. Install a VPN (ExpressVPN, NordVPN, etc.) on all devices
  2. Download offline maps from Maps.me or Google Maps
  3. Download Google Translate with offline Chinese language pack
  4. Download WeChat and register your phone number

SIM cards at Chengdu airports:

Buy a Chinese SIM card at TFU or CTU from China Mobile, China Unicom, or China Telecom. Tourist packages: ¥80–200 for 15–30 days with data. Passport required.

Useful apps:

  • WeChat — essential
  • Didi — ride-hailing (works with foreign phone)
  • Pleco — Chinese dictionary
  • Google Translate — offline language pack
  • Maps.me — offline maps

About Chengdu's Hospitals

West China Hospital (四川大学华西医院), affiliated with Sichuan University, is consistently ranked among China's top 3 hospitals nationally. It is a pioneer in neurology, neurosurgery, and organ transplantation, with one of the largest liver transplant programs in Asia. The hospital serves patients from across Western China and increasingly from overseas.

Chengdu's medical infrastructure is well-developed and efficient. West China Hospital's international liaison office has extensive experience coordinating care for foreign patients, from initial consultation through surgery and rehabilitation.
