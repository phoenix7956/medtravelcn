Kunming is the capital of Yunnan and the gateway to Southeast Asia. With direct flights from Bangkok, Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, and Yangon, it serves as a low-cost medical tourism entry point for patients from ASEAN, Myanmar, and South Asia.

Why Choose Kunming?

Kunming's mild climate (the "City of Eternal Spring"), lower altitude than other Chinese cities, and direct flights from ASEAN capitals make it ideal for SE Asian medical tourists seeking affordable tier-3 care. Kunming Medical University's hospitals have long experience with SE Asian patients and Thai/Vietnamese/English language coordinators are common.
