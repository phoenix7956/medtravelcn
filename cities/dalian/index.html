Dalian is a coastal northeast city with strong Japanese and Korean communities. Its medical centers serve large Japanese and Korean patient bases, with bilingual signage and Japan-trained specialists common.

Why Choose Dalian?

Dalian's coastal location, mild climate, and direct ferry routes to Japan and Korea make it ideal for NE Asian medical tourists. Hospitals are experienced with both Japanese and Korean patients — Japanese specialists trained at Tsukuba, Tokyo, and Seoul work at major institutions.
