Wuhan, China — capital of Hubei Province (60 million people) and central China's largest city — is home to Tongji Hospital (同济医院), one of China's most renowned names in orthopedic surgery and organ transplantation. Wuhan occupies a unique position as central China's medical capital, drawing patients from across the region for its exceptional surgical expertise.

Wuhan's hospitals gained international recognition during the COVID-19 pandemic — Tongji Hospital in particular became known worldwide for its critical care capabilities. Today, foreign patients come to Wuhan for orthopedic surgery, joint replacement, organ transplantation, and complex surgical cases — attracted by world-class outcomes at a fraction of Western prices.

Why Medical Tourists Choose Wuhan

Wuhan's medical reputation is built on surgical excellence — particularly in orthopedics and organ transplantation. Tongji Hospital's name is synonymous with orthopedic surgery throughout China, and its transplant program is one of the most active in the country.

What makes Wuhan different:

  • Tongji Hospital (同济医院) — One of China's "Big Four" hospitals; nationally ranked #3 in orthopedics and #1 in bone marrow transplantation in some surveys. Orthopedic surgery and joint replacement are particular specialties.
  • Organ transplantation — Wuhan's transplant programs (kidney, liver, heart) are among the most active in China with strong outcomes
  • Central China's medical capital — Wuhan serves 600 million+ people in central China; the scale of cases means specialists here have extraordinary experience
  • 2025: Foreign patient visits grew 49% year-over-year; patients come primarily for orthopedic surgery and organ transplant evaluation
  • Wuhan is connected by high-speed rail to Beijing (4h), Shanghai (3.5h), and Guangzhou (3h) — easy to combine with other cities

Top Hospitals That Accept Foreign Patients

Tongji Hospital, Tongji Medical College — 华中科技大学同济医院

📍 1095 Jiefang Avenue, Wuhan ☎️ +86-27-8366-2304 🌐 www.tjh.com.cn 🗂 Tertiary / National Top 3 Orthopedics 🏥 Founded 1900

Specialties: Orthopedic Surgery, Joint Replacement, Spinal Surgery, Bone Marrow Transplantation, Organ Transplantation, Oncology, Cardiology

International Services: International Medical Center with English coordinators. One of China's most experienced hospitals with foreign patients. Translation services available. Accepts direct overseas appointments.

📋 Note: Tongji Hospital is one of China's most prestigious names in orthopedics and joint replacement. If you need hip, knee, or shoulder replacement, this is one of the best — and most affordable — destinations in the world.

Renmin Hospital of Wuhan University — 武汉大学人民医院

📍 99 Zhangzhidong Road, Wuhan ☎️ +86-27-8804-1922 🌐 www.whu-hospital.com 🗂 Tertiary / Teaching Hospital 🏥 Founded 1923

Specialties: Cardiology, Neurology, Oncology, Respiratory Medicine, Emergency Medicine, Nephrology, Hepatobiliary Surgery

International Services: International Patient Services with English-speaking coordinators. Well-established foreign patient program. High-volume cardiac care center.

📋 Note: One of Wuhan's most respected general hospitals. Strong in cardiology and neurology. Located near the Yangtze River — Wuchang District, a central part of Wuhan.

Zhongnan Hospital of Wuhan University — 中南医院

📍 169 Donghu Road, Wuhan ☎️ +86-27-6781-3066 🌐 www.znhospital.cn 🗂 Tertiary / Teaching Hospital 🏥 Founded 1959

Specialties: Oncology, Radiotherapy, Hematology, Oncology Surgery, Infectious Diseases, Critical Care, Medical Imaging

International Services: International liaison office with English coordinators. Strong oncology and radiotherapy program. Accepts foreign patients for cancer treatment consultations and follow-up.

📋 Note: Particularly strong in cancer treatment — surgery, chemotherapy, and radiotherapy. Located near East Lake (Donghu) — one of Wuhan's most scenic areas, good for recovery walks.

Wuhan Union Hospital — 武汉协和医院

📍 1277 Jiefang Avenue, Wuhan ☎️ +86-27-8572-6000 🗂 Tertiary / Teaching Hospital 🏥 Founded 1866

Specialties: Cardiology, Cardiac Surgery, Thoracic Surgery, Vascular Surgery, Emergency Care, ICU, Pulmonology

International Services: International Medical Center. Well-developed cardiac surgery program. One of China's oldest hospitals (founded by a British missionary in 1866). English coordinators available.

📋 Note: One of China's oldest hospitals with a strong cardiac surgery program. Cardiothoracic surgery is a particular specialty. Located in the Hankou District — the traditional commercial center of Wuhan.

How to pay:

Most hospitals accept cash (RMB), UnionPay cards, and major credit cards. WeChat Pay and Alipay require a Chinese bank account.

How to Schedule an Appointment

Option 1: Contact the hospital's international office directly

  1. Find the international/liaison contact in the Hospital section above
  2. Email or call to request an appointment — include your diagnosis, medical summary, and preferred dates
  3. Bring your passport to registration
  4. Arrive 30–60 minutes early on appointment day

Option 2: Through a medical facilitator

Facilitators in Wuhan can help with appointment coordination, translation, and logistics for major surgical cases. Fees typically $150–350.

⚠️ What to bring to your appointment:

  • Passport (required)
  • All previous medical records, imaging CDs, and X-rays
  • List of current medications
  • For orthopedic cases: bring any previous orthopedic imaging and surgical records

Language tips:

  • Download Google Translate with offline Chinese language pack
  • Pleco dictionary for medical terminology
  • WeChat has built-in translation features
  • Tongji Hospital has dedicated coordinators for international patients

Getting to Wuhan from the US

Wuhan Tianhe International Airport (WUH) has limited direct US flights. Most travelers connect through Shanghai, Seoul, Tokyo, or Hong Kong.

RouteFlight DurationRound-Trip Economy
LAX → WUH (via ICN/NRT/SHA)~16–19 hours total$700–1,300
SFO → WUH (via Seoul/Shanghai)~15–18 hours total$650–1,200
SEA → WUH (via Tokyo)~14–17 hours total$650–1,200

From the airport to the city:

  • Metro Line 2 (地铁2号线) — Connects Wuhan Tianhe Airport to central Wuhan (about 45–60 minutes to Hankou/Wuchang). ¥5–10.
  • Taxi — ¥80–150 to central Wuhan. About 30–50 minutes depending on traffic.
  • Didi — Request via app once you have data.

High-speed rail connections:

Wuhan is China's central high-speed rail hub. Fast connections to:

  • Beijing: ~4 hours (G-train)
  • Shanghai: ~3.5 hours (G-train)
  • Guangzhou: ~3 hours (G-train)
  • Nanjing: ~2 hours (G-train)

Visa:

US citizens need a tourist visa (L visa). Apply at the Chinese Visa Application Service Center. Processing: 4–5 business days. Cost: $140.

Money & Payments

Currency: Chinese Yuan (RMB / ¥)

  • Cash: RMB works everywhere
  • Cards: Visa and Mastercard accepted at most hospitals and hotels
  • WeChat Pay & Alipay: Most convenient — Chinese bank account recommended

Exchange money:

  • Best rates: ATM withdrawals with Charles Schwab, Revolut, or Wise debit cards
  • Airport: Available but poor rates
  • Banks: ICBC and China Construction Bank near hospitals offer fair rates

Cost of living reference (Wuhan):

  • Cheap meal: ¥15–35 ($2–5)
  • Mid-range restaurant: ¥60–150 ($9–22)
  • Subway: ¥2–8 ($0.30–1.20)
  • Taxi (per km): ¥2–3.5 ($0.30–0.50)

Staying Connected in China

Internet: China uses blocked internet. Install a VPN before arrival.

Before you leave the US:

  1. Install a VPN (ExpressVPN, NordVPN, etc.) on all devices
  2. Download offline maps from Maps.me or Google Maps
  3. Download Google Translate with offline Chinese language pack
  4. Download WeChat and register your phone number

SIM cards at Wuhan airport:

Buy a Chinese SIM card at WUH from China Mobile, China Unicom, or China Telecom. Tourist packages: ¥80–200 for 15–30 days with data. Passport required.

Useful apps:

  • WeChat — essential
  • Didi — ride-hailing (works with foreign phone)
  • Pleco — Chinese dictionary
  • Google Translate — offline language pack
  • Maps.me — offline maps

Important Tips for First-Time Visitors

Ready to Get Started?

Contact Tongji Hospital's international office directly for orthopedic surgery and organ transplant consultations. Wuhan's surgical expertise is among the best in China.

Before you go:

  • Apply for your Chinese tourist visa (L visa)
  • Notify your bank you'll be in China
  • Install and test a VPN before arrival
  • Download WeChat and register your phone number
  • Bring all medical records, imaging CDs, and surgical history
  • For orthopedic cases: bring previous X-rays, MRI CDs, and orthopedic reports
  • Save hospital addresses in Chinese characters
  • Bring a debit card with no foreign ATM fees

At the hospital:

  • Bring your passport — required
  • Arrive 30–60 minutes early
  • Keep all receipts and medical records
  • Ask for English-language summaries of your treatment plan

🚨 Emergencies:

  • Ambulance: 120
  • Police: 110

After you return home:

  • Keep all medical records and imaging CDs
  • Follow up with your US doctor
  • Contact Wuhan hospitals directly for follow-up needs
