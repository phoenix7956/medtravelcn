Xian, China — home to the Terracotta Warriors and the ancient Silk Road — is one of central-western China's most important medical destinations. The city is home to Xijing Hospital (西京医院), one of China's most renowned institutions for plastic and cosmetic surgery, as well as excellent general and cardiac care at Xi'an Jiaotong University-affiliated hospitals.

Xian's combination of world-class medical facilities and one of China's most compelling tourism destinations makes it unique: patients can combine medical treatment with exploring 3,000 years of Chinese civilization, from the Ancient City Wall to the Big Wild Goose Pagoda.

Why Medical Tourists Choose Xian

Xian occupies a special niche in China's medical landscape — particularly for plastic and cosmetic surgery, where Xijing Hospital's reputation rivals the best in Asia. For other specialties, Xian's Jiaotong University-affiliated hospitals offer excellent care at very competitive prices.

What makes Xian different:

  • Xijing Hospital (西京医院 / Fourth Military Medical University) — One of China's top hospitals for plastic and cosmetic surgery; nationally renowned for rhinoplasty, facial contouring, and reconstructive surgery. Also excellent in cardiology and cardiac surgery.
  • Xi'an Jiaotong University First Affiliated Hospital (西安交大一附院) — General tertiary care with particular strength in cardiology, nephrology, and endocrinology
  • Plastic surgery at a fraction of Western cost — rhinoplasty, facelifts, breast procedures, and eyelid surgery performed by some of China's most experienced surgeons at 60–80% below US prices
  • 2025: Foreign patient visits grew 53% year-over-year; significant growth in cosmetic surgery and health checkup tourism
  • Xian is a UNESCO World Heritage city — Terracotta Warriors, City Wall, Muslim Quarter — recovery can include daily sightseeing

Top Hospitals That Accept Foreign Patients

Xijing Hospital, Fourth Military Medical University — 西京医院

While You Wait — Explore the City

Many procedures and diagnostic tests (MRI, CT, blood work) can be scheduled within 3–7 days. While you wait for results or pre-procedure assessments, you can explore the city. Your hospital liaison office can help plan sightseeing between appointments.

How to pay:

Most hospitals accept cash (RMB), UnionPay cards, and major credit cards. WeChat Pay and Alipay require a Chinese bank account.

How to Schedule an Appointment

Option 1: Contact the hospital's international office directly

  1. Find the international/liaison contact in the Hospital section above
  2. Email or call to request an appointment — for cosmetic surgery, include photos and desired procedure descriptions
  3. Bring your passport to registration

Option 2: Through a medical facilitator

Facilitators familiar with Xijing Hospital's cosmetic surgery department can help with consultation arrangement, translation, and post-op care coordination. Fees typically $150–400.

⚠️ What to bring to your appointment:

  • Passport (required)
  • For cosmetic surgery: bring reference photos and a clear description of your goals
  • All previous medical records
  • List of current medications

Getting to Xian from the US

Xian Xianyang International Airport (XIY) has limited direct US flights. Most travelers connect through Shanghai, Seoul, or Tokyo.

RouteFlight DurationRound-Trip Economy
LAX → XIY (via SHA/ICN)~17–20 hours total$700–1,300
SFO → XIY (via Seoul)~15–18 hours total$650–1,200
SEA → XIY (via Tokyo)~15–17 hours total$650–1,200

From the airport to the city:

  • Metro Line 14 (地铁14号线) — Connects XIY to central Xian (about 45–60 minutes to North Station / City Center). ¥7–15.
  • Taxi — ¥100–180 to central Xian. About 40–60 minutes.
  • Didi — Request via app once you have data.

Visa:

US citizens need a tourist visa (L visa). Apply at the Chinese Visa Application Service Center. Processing: 4–5 business days. Cost: $140. Xian participates in the 72-hour and 144-hour visa-free transit policy for many nationalities.

Money & Payments

Currency: Chinese Yuan (RMB / ¥)

  • Cash: RMB works everywhere
  • Cards: Visa and Mastercard accepted at hospitals and hotels
  • WeChat Pay & Alipay: Most convenient — Chinese bank account recommended

Exchange money:

  • Best rates: ATM withdrawals with Charles Schwab, Revolut, or Wise debit cards
  • Airport: Available but poor rates

Cost of living reference (Xian):

  • Cheap meal: ¥15–35 ($2–5)
  • Mid-range restaurant: ¥60–150 ($9–22)
  • Subway: ¥2–8 ($0.30–1.20)
  • Taxi (per km): ¥2–3.5 ($0.30–0.50)

Staying Connected in China

Internet: China uses blocked internet. Install a VPN before arrival.

Before you leave the US:

  1. Install a VPN on all devices
  2. Download offline maps from Maps.me or Google Maps
  3. Download Google Translate with offline Chinese language pack
  4. Download WeChat and register your phone number

SIM cards at Xian airport:

Buy a Chinese SIM card at XIY from China Mobile, China Unicom, or China Telecom. Tourist packages: ¥80–200 for 15–30 days with data.

Useful apps:

  • WeChat — essential
  • Didi — ride-hailing (works with foreign phone)
  • Pleco — Chinese dictionary
  • Google Translate — offline language pack
  • Maps.me — offline maps

About Xi'an's Hospitals

Xi'an's First Affiliated Hospital (西安交通大学第一附属医院) and Xijing Hospital (西京医院, Fourth Military Medical University) are nationally leading institutions. Xijing Hospital is widely recognized as China's #1 hospital for plastic and reconstructive surgery, burn treatment, and cosmetic surgery, attracting patients from across the country and overseas.

Xi'an's medical sector also has strong expertise in digestive diseases, cardiovascular care, and general surgery. The city's established international airport and high-speed rail connections make it easily accessible from most Chinese cities.
