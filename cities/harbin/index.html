Harbin is the largest city in northeast China with Russian, Korean, and Japanese cultural ties. Harbin Medical University hospitals are large tier-3 centers experienced in treating Russian patients from across the border — interpreters for Russian commonly available.

Why Choose Harbin?

Harbin offers tier-3 care at China's lowest prices for major cities. Cross-border Russian patients have driven hospitals to maintain Russian-language coordinators, Korean and Japanese translation is also widely supported. Direct flights from Vladivostok, Khabarovsk, Seoul, and Tokyo make access easy.
