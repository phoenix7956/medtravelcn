Chongqing, China — China's largest municipality by area (31 million people) and one of its most geographically dramatic cities — sits at the confluence of the Yangtze and Jialing Rivers, surrounded by mountains. It is also a major medical destination, home to Southwest Hospital (西南医院), which is nationally renowned for burns treatment and plastic/reconstructive surgery, as well as excellent general and emergency care.

Chongqing's hospitals serve as the primary medical referral center for southwestern China — a region of 300+ million people. The scale of cases means specialists here have extraordinary experience in complex conditions. Chongqing also has China's most extensive metro system and a rapidly growing international airport, making access increasingly convenient.

Why Medical Tourists Choose Chongqing

Chongqing's medical niche is centered on burns treatment, reconstructive surgery, and complex trauma care — where Southwest Hospital's expertise is unmatched in western China. For general medical care, Chongqing's hospitals offer excellent quality at very competitive prices.

What makes Chongqing different:

  • Southwest Hospital (西南医院) — Nationally renowned for burns treatment, plastic surgery, and reconstructive surgery. One of China's leading centers for major burns care and complex wound reconstruction. Also excellent in general surgery and trauma care.
  • Chongqing Medical University First Affiliated Hospital (重医附一院) — Major general hospital with strong programs in neurology, cardiology, and oncology
  • Daping Hospital (大坪医院) — Well-regarded for emergency medicine, trauma care, and military medicine. Also strong in cardiology and vascular surgery.
  • 2025: Foreign patient visits grew 44% year-over-year; most patients come for burns treatment, reconstructive surgery, and complex diagnostics

Southwest Hospital — 西南医院

How to pay:

Most hospitals accept cash (RMB), UnionPay cards, and major credit cards. WeChat Pay and Alipay require a Chinese bank account.

How to Schedule an Appointment

Option 1: Contact the hospital's international office directly

  1. Find the international/liaison contact in the Hospital section above
  2. Email or call to request an appointment — for burns/reconstructive cases, include description of condition
  3. Bring your passport to registration

Option 2: Through a medical facilitator

Facilitators can help coordinate appointments at Chongqing's major hospitals, particularly for complex cases. Fees typically $150–350.

⚠️ What to bring to your appointment:

  • Passport (required)
  • All previous medical records and imaging CDs
  • List of current medications
  • For burns/reconstructive cases: bring photos and previous treatment records if available

Getting to Chongqing from the US

Chongqing Jiangbei International Airport (CKG) is a major hub with growing international connections. Most US travelers connect through Shanghai, Seoul, or Tokyo.

RouteFlight DurationRound-Trip Economy
LAX → CKG (via SHA/ICN)~18–21 hours total$700–1,300
SFO → CKG (via Seoul)~16–19 hours total$650–1,200
SEA → CKG (via Tokyo)~15–18 hours total$650–1,200

From the airport to the city:

  • Metro Line 3/10 (地铁3/10号线) — Connects CKG to central Chongqing. About 40–50 minutes to central stations. ¥5–12.
  • Taxi — ¥80–150 to central Chongqing. About 30–50 minutes.
  • Didi — Request via app once you have data.

Visa:

US citizens need a tourist visa (L visa). Apply at the Chinese Visa Application Service Center. Processing: 4–5 business days. Cost: $140.

Money & Payments

Currency: Chinese Yuan (RMB / ¥)

  • Cash: RMB works everywhere
  • Cards: Visa and Mastercard accepted at most hospitals and hotels
  • WeChat Pay & Alipay: Most convenient — Chinese bank account recommended

Exchange money:

  • Best rates: ATM withdrawals with Charles Schwab, Revolut, or Wise debit cards
  • Airport: Available but poor rates

Cost of living reference (Chongqing):

  • Subway: ¥2–7 ($0.30–1.00)

Staying Connected in China

Internet: China uses blocked internet. Install a VPN before arrival.

Before you leave the US:

  1. Install a VPN on all devices
  2. Download offline maps from Maps.me or Google Maps
  3. Download Google Translate with offline Chinese language pack
  4. Download WeChat and register your phone number

SIM cards at Chongqing airport:

Buy a Chinese SIM card at CKG from China Mobile, China Unicom, or China Telecom. Tourist packages: ¥80–200 for 15–30 days with data.

Useful apps:

  • WeChat — essential
  • Didi — ride-hailing (works with foreign phone)
  • Pleco — Chinese dictionary
  • Google Translate — offline language pack
  • Maps.me — offline maps

Important Tips for First-Time Visitors

Ready to Get Started?

Contact Southwest Hospital's international office directly for burns treatment and reconstructive surgery consultations. Chongqing offers excellent care at some of the lowest prices among China's major cities.

Before you go:

  • Apply for your Chinese tourist visa (L visa)
  • Notify your bank you'll be in China
  • Install and test a VPN before arrival
  • Download WeChat and register your phone number
  • Bring all medical records and imaging CDs
  • For burns/reconstructive cases: bring photos and previous treatment records
  • Save hospital addresses in Chinese characters
  • Bring a debit card with no foreign ATM fees

At the hospital:

  • Bring your passport — required
  • Arrive 30–60 minutes early
  • Keep all receipts and medical records
  • Ask for English-language summaries of your treatment plan

🚨 Emergencies:

  • Ambulance: 120
  • Police: 110

After you return home:

  • Keep all medical records and imaging CDs
  • Follow up with your US doctor
  • Contact Chongqing hospitals directly for follow-up needs
