Xiamen is a coastal city in Fujian with strong ties to overseas Chinese communities in Southeast Asia. Its flagship hospital became the first JCI-academic medical center in Fujian, drawing patients from the Philippines, Malaysia, Indonesia, and Singapore.

Why Choose Xiamen?

Xiamen offers internationally accredited care at tier-3 prices 50-70% below Western standards. The city has decades of experience serving overseas Chinese patients and is increasingly the entry point for Southeast Asian medical tourists. Direct flights from Bangkok, Manila, Kuala Lumpur, and Singapore make access easy.
