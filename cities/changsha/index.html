Changsha is home to Xiangya — one of China's oldest and most respected tier-1 hospital systems, founded in 1906 by Yale-China Association. The city combines heritage medical excellence with modern infrastructure at some of China's lowest tier-3 prices.

Why Choose Changsha?

Changsha's Xiangya hospital system has a century-long history of international cooperation. The three Xiangya hospitals together handle tens of millions of outpatient visits per year. Their English-friendly international departments are well-established. Prices are typically 60-80% below US rates.
