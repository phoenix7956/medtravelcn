Jinan is the capital of Shandong and home to Qilu Hospital — among China's most historically significant tier-3 centers. The city draws Russian and Korean medical tourists seeking affordable specialty care with strong hospital English support.

Why Choose Jinan?

Jinan sits at the crossroads of Korean, Japanese, and Russian patient flows via Shandong's coastal proximity. Qilu Hospital has 130+ years of history with established English-language international departments. Tier-3 prices are 50-70% below Western standards.
