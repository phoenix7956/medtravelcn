How It Works

From first inquiry to recovery — how international patients travel to China for affordable medical care.

Most international patients complete their medical journey in 3 simple steps. We help you at every stage.

01

Explore & Inquire

Browse our city pages to learn about hospitals, procedures, and estimated costs. When you're ready, send us a message with:

  • Procedure or condition you're seeking treatment for
  • Preferred city (or open to recommendations)
  • Timeline — when do you want to travel?
  • Any medical records or reports you already have

We typically respond within 24 hours with hospital options and cost estimates tailored to your case.

🔍
02

Book & Prepare

Once you choose a hospital and procedure:

  • We help you arrange the appointment with the hospital's international patient center
  • You receive a medical invitation letter (for visa support if needed)
  • We guide you through visa applications, flight bookings, and accommodation options near the hospital
  • You receive a pre-travel checklist — what to bring, how to prepare

We stay in touch via email and WeChat so you always have a point of contact.

📋
03

Travel & Treatment

When you arrive in China:

  • We or our local partners meet you at the airport (optional)
  • Hospital transfer arranged — private car or taxi, depending on your preference
  • Hospital admission — international patient center staff check you in
  • Your procedure or consultation takes place as scheduled
  • Recovery monitoring in-hospital, then discharge with a full follow-up care plan
  • We'll help arrange any local accommodation for recovery stays if needed
✈️
04

Return & Follow-Up

Before you leave China:

  • You receive complete medical records in English (discharge summary, imaging, prescriptions)
  • Digital copies of all scans on USB or CD
  • A detailed follow-up care plan to share with your doctor back home
  • We remain available for remote consultation if you have questions after returning

Your care doesn't end when you board the plane home.

🏥

Important Notes

  • This website is an information resource only. We do not arrange medical appointments or provide medical services.
  • All medical decisions should be made in consultation with your treating physician and home-country doctor.
  • Prices listed on this site are estimates only. Actual costs depend on the hospital, surgeon, materials used, and individual case. Always get a formal quote.
  • Verify all information directly with your chosen hospital before making travel arrangements.
